Sudsy: Idle Car Wash Tycoon · Inguqulo 1.0 · Isebenza kusukela mhla zingama-21 kuNovemba 2026

Le nqubomgomo ichaza ukuthi yimuphi idatha umdlalo i-Sudsy: Idle Car Wash Tycoon ("umdlalo") oyicubungulayo, kungani, nokuthi ungenzani ngakho. Uma ukuhumusha kwehlukile kunenguqulo yesiNgisi, kusebenza inguqulo yesiNgisi.

Singobani

Umdlalo ushicilelwa yi-Lagom Labs ("thina"). Oxhumana naye: lagomlabs@gmail.com.

Lokho umdlalo okuqoqayo

  • Izehlakalo zomdlalo (Firebase Analytics). Izehlakalo ezifana nokugeza okuqediwe (imoto, izinga, ngesandla noma ngethimba, isikhathi esithathiwe), ukuthuthukiswa kweziteshi, ukuqashwa kwabasebenzi, izehlakalo ezibukhoma, izinyathelo zesifundo, okunikezwayo kwezikhangiso zomvuzo nokubukwa kwazo kuze kube sekugcineni, ukubukwa kwesitolo nokuthenga (i-ID yomkhiqizo), ukuthengiswa kwe-franchise, inzuzo yangaphandle kwe-inthanethi eqoqiwe, imiphumela yokulondoloza emafini kanye nemvume yezaziso nokuvulwa kwazo, kanye ne-ID engahleliwe yesibonelo sohlelo lokusebenza nolwazi oluyisisekelo ngedivayisi nohlelo lokusebenza (imodeli, inguqulo ye-OS, ulimi, izwe elitholwe ekhelini le-IP, inguqulo yohlelo lokusebenza) futhi, ekuqaleni kokuqala, umthombo wokufaka we-Google Play (ikhasi lesitolo noma umkhankaso oholele ekufakweni). Siphinde sibhale lapho umdlalo ucela ukulinganiselwa esitolo. Ukuqoqwa kuvaliwe kuze kube yilapho isinyathelo semvume sikuvumela, futhi ungakuvala noma nini ku-Izilungiselelo → Izibalo zokusebenzisa. Ukuhlaziywa akuqoqi i-ID yakho yesikhangiso noma i-Android ID.
  • Imibiko yokuphahlazeka (Firebase Crashlytics). Lapho umdlalo uphahlazeka: umkhondo wokuphahlazeka, imodeli yedivayisi, inguqulo ye-OS, inkumbulo ekhululekile kanye ne-ID engahleliwe yokufaka ye-Crashlytics, ukuze sithole futhi silungise amaphutha.
  • Izilungiselelo zesikude (Firebase Remote Config). Umdlalo ulanda amanani okulinganisa umdlalo; lokhu kusebenzisa i-ID engahleliwe yokufaka ye-Firebase.
  • Izikhangiso (Google AdMob). Umdlalo ubonisa kuphela izikhangiso zomvuzo okhetha ukuzibuka. Ukuze izibonise, i-AdMob icubungula i-ID yesikhangiso yedivayisi yakho, ikheli le-IP nolwazi lwedivayisi. Ku-EEA, e-UK nakwezinye izifunda lapho kudingeka khona, ifomu lemvume le-Google likubuza kuqala; ngaphandle kwemvume yakho izikhangiso azenzelwa wena ngqo.
  • Ukulondoloza emafini (Google Play Games Services). Uma ungene ku-Google Play Games, i-ID yakho yomdlali ye-Play Games nenqubekela phambili yakho yomdlalo (ifayela lokulondoloza) kugcinwa e-akhawuntini yakho ye-Google ukuze uqhubeke kwenye idivayisi.
  • Ukuthenga (Google Play Billing). Izinkokhelo ziphathwa ngokuphelele yi-Google Play. Umdlalo uthola kuphela irekhodi lokuthenga (i-ID yomkhiqizo, i-oda nethokheni yokuthenga) futhi awulokothi ubone imininingwane yekhadi lakho noma yasebhange.
  • Izaziso (Firebase Cloud Messaging). Kuphela uma uvumela izaziso, idivayisi ithola ithokheni yemiyalezo futhi ibhaliselwe isihloko sezindaba solimi lwakho lomdlalo (isibonelo "lang_zu"). Izikhumbuzo ezifana nethi "Ibhokisi lemali ligcwele!" zihlelwa kudivayisi yakho futhi azithumeli idatha.
  • Ukulinganisa (Google Play In-App Review). Umdlalo ungase ubonise ikhadi lokulinganisa le-Google Play ngemuva komzuzu wenjabulo. I-Google inquma ukuthi liyavela yini; umdlalo awulokothi wazi ukuthi ulinganisile yini noma kanjani.
  • Kudivayisi yakho. Inqubekela phambili yakho nezilungiselelo zakho kugcinwa kudivayisi.

Lokho umdlalo ongakuqoqi

Igama lakho, ikheli le-imeyili, inombolo yocingo, indawo oyiyo ngqo, oxhumana nabo, izithombe, amafayela noma imakrofoni. Umdlalo awuceli lezi zimvume. Umdlalo ngokwawo ucela: ukufinyelela ku-inthanethi, isimo senethiwekhi, ukudlidliza, izaziso (Android 13+, uyazikhethela), ukuqala kabusha izikhumbuzo ngemuva kokuqalisa kabusha idivayisi, i-ID yesikhangiso (yezikhangiso) kanye nokukhokha kwe-Google Play. Imitapo yolwazi ye-Google ngaphakathi komdlalo yengeza: ukugcina idivayisi iphapheme isikhashana ngemisebenzi yangemuva, ukwamukela imiyalezo ye-push, ukufunda umthombo wokufaka we-Play, ama-API e-Android ezinsizakalo zezikhangiso zokubhekisela kanye ne-ID yesikhangiso, isimo esiyisisekelo socingo (uhlobo lwenethiwekhi, olusetshenziswa izikhangiso) kanye nensizakalo yangaphambili yomsebenzi wangemuva we-Google. Akukho kulokhu okukubuza lutho esikrinini futhi akukho okunikeza ukufinyelela ezingcingweni zakho, emiyalezweni noma emafayeleni akho.

Kungani sikusebenzisa

Ukuze siqhube umdlalo futhi silondoloze inqubekela phambili yakho, siyibuyise kudivayisi entsha, sithole futhi silungise ukuphahlazeka, silinganise umdlalo, sibonise izikhangiso zomvuzo ozikhethayo futhi sikulethele lokho okuthengayo.

Izinkampani zangaphandle

Zonke izinsizakalo ezingenhla zinikezwa yi-Google: Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config kanye ne-Cloud Messaging (firebase.google.com/support/privacy), AdMob (policies.google.com/technologies/ads), Google Play Games Services kanye ne-Google Play Billing (policies.google.com/privacy). Asiyidayisi idatha yakho futhi asiyabelani nomunye umuntu.

Imvume nokukhetha kwakho

  • Ifomu lemvume livela ekuqaleni kokuqala lapho kudingeka khona; liphinde ulivule noma nini ku-**Izilungiselelo → Okuthandwayo kobumfihlo**.
  • Vala izibalo zokusebenzisa ku-Izilungiselelo → Izibalo zokusebenzisa.
  • Vala izaziso ngesiteshi ku-Izilungiselelo noma kuzilungiselelo ze-Android.
  • Phuma ezikhangisweni ezenzelwe wena ngqo kuzilungiselelo zakho ze-Android (Google → Izikhangiso).

Ukugcina nokususa idatha

  • Izilungiselelo → Susa idatha yami isusa inqubekela phambili kule divayisi nasekulondolozeni kwakho emafini kwe-Play Games, isusa ithokheni yezaziso futhi isetha kabusha i-ID yakho yokuhlaziya. Okuthengiwe kuhlala ne-akhawunti yakho ye-Google.
  • Idatha yokuhlaziya igcinwa izinyanga ezingu-14 kuphela; imibiko yokuphahlazeka izinsuku ezingu-90.
  • Ukusicela ukuthi sisuse idatha esingayixhumanisa nawe, bhalela ku-lagomlabs@gmail.com (faka igama lakho lomdlali we-Play Games uma usebenzisa ukulondoloza emafini). Siphendula kungakapheli izinsuku ezingu-30.

Izingane

Bhalela ku-lagomlabs@gmail.com. Ungase futhi ukhalaze kuziphathimandla zakho zokuvikelwa kwedatha.
