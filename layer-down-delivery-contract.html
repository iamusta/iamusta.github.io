2. Product definition (verified)

Layer Down is a casual drilling game: in short runs the player drills down through the layers of a site with five timing moments (spin-up, cooling, layer swap, vein grab, retract), brings ore back up, sells it for coins at once or later on an in-game market, and upgrades the drill and the mining company to reach each site's core. All gameplay runs on the device and works offline; there are no accounts of its own. With Google Play Games the player's progress can be backed up as a saved game and achievements are shown in Google Play Games. Optional rewarded ads and occasional interstitial ads are served by Google AdMob, optional items are sold through Google Play, and usage statistics and crash reports go to Google Firebase.

2.2 Gameplay (verified)

  • Five timing moments in every run: spin-up, cooling vents, layer swaps, vein grabs and the retract
  • From the second layer on, cave creatures crawl at the drill; a tap on their side knocks them off (a well-timed tap drops ore), and one that reaches the drill slows it and heats it until it is shaken off
  • Eight upgrades: drill bit, motor, cooling, fuel tank and cargo hold, plus the refinery, warehouse and workers of the company
  • 6 sites from Pinewood Forest (1,000 m) to Asteroid K-7 (125,000 m), each with 7 layers ending in the core; reaching a site's core unlocks the next site
  • 16 ores, sold right after a run or stored in the silo and sold later on the market, whose prices move over time; the market trades only in-game coins
  • 16 drill models with their own bonuses
  • Daily missions with a bonus chest, a 7-day daily reward, 44 achievements with coin rewards
  • New Charter (from Frostcap Polar on): the player restarts equipment, buildings, coins, silo and sites for Charter Stars that add +10% income and +2% drill speed each; drills, records, achievements and purchases are kept
  • If the bit overheats, the run ends and 60% of the cargo is kept, unless the player continues for a rewarded ad; retracting too late loses up to 50% of the cargo
  • Earnings while the game is closed, for 2 to 12 hours depending on the warehouse, which can be doubled with a rewarded ad
  • Local reminders at most twice a day, which the player can turn off

2.4 Languages (77 in the language menu)

The language menu offers 77 languages, including Arabic, Persian, Hebrew and Urdu (right-to-left) and Chinese, Japanese and Korean. In version 0.3.0 only English is complete; text that is not translated yet is shown in English. The store listing covers 1 Google Play store language.

2.5 Permissions, ads & analytics

  • Permissions: INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, VIBRATE, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, com.android.vending.BILLING, plus the advertising-ID and technical permissions added by Google libraries
  • Ads: Google AdMob — rewarded ads only when the player chooses to watch one (×2 cargo, ×2 offline earnings, continue after an overheat, +50% fuel, an upgrade the player cannot afford yet, by default up to 8 times a day, free coins 3 times a day); interstitial ads on the way back from a run's result (not before 5 runs are finished, by default at most once every 3 runs, removed by Remove Ads); Google's consent form in the EEA, UK and Switzerland; content rated PG
  • Analytics: Google Firebase Analytics (after the consent step; can be turned off in Settings), Crashlytics crash reports, Remote Config, Cloud Messaging announcement topics
  • Account & cloud: no own accounts; optional Google Play Games sign-in for the cloud saved game and achievements

3. Delivery of purchased items

  • Seller of record: purchases are made through Google Play; Google processes the payment under the Google Play Terms of Service.
  • Delivery: items are digital and are delivered to the player's game immediately after Google Play confirms the payment. Permanent items are acknowledged and consumable items are consumed with Google Play at delivery.
  • Permanent items (starter_pack, no_ads, vip_crew, double_coins) belong to the Google account used for the purchase and are restored when the game is reinstalled on the same Google account (when the game connects to Google Play, or Shop → Restore purchases): no interstitials, the VIP and ×2 coin bonuses and the Pioneer and Royal Driller drills come back. The coins and ×2 runs in the Starter Pack and the VIP coin gift are delivered once per saved game; progress restored from Google Play Games keeps what was already delivered.
  • Pending payments (for example cash payments) are delivered when Google Play confirms them, including the next time the game is opened.
  • Coins are an in-game virtual currency with no cash value. Coins and ores cannot be exchanged for money, transferred or traded outside the game, and are stored on the device: clearing app data or uninstalling removes them, unless the progress is restored from the player's Google Play Games saved game.
  • Right of withdrawal: because digital content is delivered immediately at the player's request, the right of withdrawal ends once delivery begins, to the extent permitted by applicable law (including EU consumer law and the Turkish Regulation on Distance Contracts, Art. 15(ğ)).
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy. If an item was paid for but not delivered, contact us with the Google Play order number (GPA.…) and we will deliver it or help you obtain a refund.
