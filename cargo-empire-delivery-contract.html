1. Purpose

This agreement records what Cargo Empire v1.0.0 delivers at release and how purchased digital items are delivered. It is a product scope, acceptance and delivery document — not legal advice.

2. Product definition (verified)

Cargo Empire is a one-handed vertical idle logistics tycoon: the player founds a cargo company, delivers parcels on a city map and grows a fleet from a moto courier to cargo planes. All gameplay runs on the device; there are no accounts, no cloud save and no analytics. Optional rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob, and optional items are sold through Google Play.

2.2 Gameplay (verified)

  • Tap a route to send a delivery; managers automate routes
  • 7 vehicles on road, rail, sea and air routes: Moto Courier, Van, Truck, Semi Fleet, Freight Train, Container Ship, Cargo Plane
  • 47 upgrades, 21 manager cards with levels, 3 regions unlocked by going public, investors that raise income permanently
  • Rush orders, depot fires, timed contracts, daily rewards with streaks, a 28-day Expedition Pass, weekly events and a 20-company league
  • Earnings while the game is closed: the first hour free, up to 8 more hours with a rewarded ad or always with Nonstop Fleet
  • Real-time day and night, seasons and weather on the map

2.4 Languages (72)

Türkçe, English and 70 more: every language supported by Google Play, including Arabic, Persian, Hebrew and Urdu (right-to-left) and Chinese, Japanese and Korean.

2.5 Permissions, ads & analytics

  • Permissions: INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, VIBRATE, com.android.vending.BILLING (Google Play purchases), plus advertising-ID and technical permissions added by Google libraries
  • Ads: rewarded only (Google AdMob), shown only when the player chooses to watch one; Google's consent form in the EEA, UK and Switzerland; child-directed, G-rated ads for the under-13 age group
  • Analytics: none
  • Account & cloud: none — progress is saved locally on the device; a backup code can be copied in Settings

3. Delivery of purchased items

  • Seller of record: purchases are made through Google Play; Google processes the payment under the Google Play Terms of Service.
  • Delivery: items are digital and are delivered to the player's game immediately after Google Play confirms the payment. Permanent items are acknowledged and consumable gem packs are consumed with Google Play at delivery.
  • Permanent items (starter, always_on) belong to the Google account used for the purchase. They are restored automatically when the game is reinstalled on the same Google account; the gems and Turbo time in the Starter Pack are not granted again. The Expedition Pass Premium applies to the season in which it was bought.
  • Pending payments (for example cash payments) are delivered when Google Play confirms them, including the next time the game is opened.
  • Gems are an in-game virtual currency with no cash value. They cannot be exchanged for money, transferred or traded, and are stored on the device: clearing app data or uninstalling removes them unless a save backup is restored.
  • Right of withdrawal: because digital content is delivered immediately at the player's request, the right of withdrawal ends once delivery begins, to the extent permitted by applicable law (including EU consumer law and Turkish Regulation on Distance Contracts, Art. 15(ğ)).
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy. If an item was paid for but not delivered, contact us with the Google Play order number (GPA.…) and we will deliver it or help you obtain a refund.
