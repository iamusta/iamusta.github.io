Privacy Policy

Sudsy: Idle Car Wash Tycoon · Version 1.0 · Effective 21 November 2026

This policy explains what data the game Sudsy: Idle Car Wash Tycoon (the "game") processes, why, and what you can do about it. If a translation differs from the English version, the English version applies.

Who we are

The game is published by Lagom Labs ("we", "us"). Contact: lagomlabs@gmail.com.

What the game collects

  • Game events (Firebase Analytics). Events such as finished washes (car, quality, hand or crew, duration), station upgrades, crew hires, live events, tutorial steps, rewarded-ad offers and completions, shop views and purchases (product ID), franchise sales, offline earnings collected, cloud save results and notification permission and opens, together with a random app-instance ID and basic device and app information (model, OS version, language, country derived from the IP address, app version) and, on the first start, the Google Play install referrer (which store page or campaign led to the install). We also log when the game asks for a store rating. Collection is off until the consent step allows it, and you can switch it off at any time in Settings → Usage statistics. Analytics does not collect your advertising ID or Android ID.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). When the game crashes: the crash trace, device model, OS version, free memory and a random Crashlytics installation ID, to find and fix bugs.
  • Remote settings (Firebase Remote Config). The game downloads game-balance values; this uses a random Firebase installation ID.
  • Ads (Google AdMob). The game only shows rewarded ads that you choose to watch. To show them, AdMob processes your device's advertising ID, IP address and device information. In the EEA, the UK and other regions where it is required, Google's consent form asks you first; without your consent ads are non-personalized.
  • Cloud save (Google Play Games Services). If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your Play Games player ID and your game progress (a save file) are stored in your Google account so you can continue on another device.
  • Purchases (Google Play Billing). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. The game receives only a purchase record (product ID, order and purchase token) and never sees your card or bank details.
  • Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging). Only if you allow notifications, the device gets a messaging token and is subscribed to a news topic for your game language (for example "lang_en"). Reminders such as "cash box full" are planned on your device and send no data.
  • Ratings (Google Play In-App Review). The game may show Google Play's rating card after a happy moment. Google decides whether it appears; the game never learns whether or how you rated.
  • On your device. Your progress and settings are stored on the device.

What the game does not collect

Your name, e-mail address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, files or microphone. The game does not ask for these permissions. The game itself asks for: internet access, network state, vibration, notifications (Android 13+, optional), restarting reminders after a reboot, the advertising ID (for ads) and Google Play billing. Google's libraries inside the game add: keeping the device awake briefly for background tasks, receiving push messages, reading the Play install referrer, the Android ad-services attribution and ad ID APIs, the basic phone state (network type, used by ads) and a foreground service for Google's background work. None of these asks you for anything on screen and none gives access to your calls, messages or files.

Why we use it

To run the game and save your progress, to restore it on a new device, to find and fix crashes, to balance the game, to show the rewarded ads you choose and to deliver what you buy.

Third parties

All services above are provided by Google: Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config and Cloud Messaging (firebase.google.com/support/privacy), AdMob (policies.google.com/technologies/ads), Google Play Games Services and Google Play Billing (policies.google.com/privacy). We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone else.

Consent and your choices

  • The consent form appears on first launch where required; reopen it any time in Settings → Privacy preferences.
  • Turn off usage statistics in Settings → Usage statistics.
  • Turn notifications off by channel in Settings or in Android's settings.
  • Opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Google → Ads).

Keeping and deleting data

  • Settings → Delete my data deletes the progress on this device and in your Play Games cloud save, deletes the notification token and resets your analytics ID. Purchases stay with your Google account.
  • Analytics data is kept for at most 14 months; crash reports for 90 days.
  • To ask us to delete data we can link to you, write to lagomlabs@gmail.com (include your Play Games player name if you use cloud save). We answer within 30 days.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and is rated for ages 13 and over.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the Turkish KVKK, the EU/UK GDPR or the California CCPA/CPRA) you have the right to access, correct, delete or move your data, to object to or restrict processing and to withdraw consent. Write to lagomlabs@gmail.com. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

We will update this policy when the game changes what it processes. The version and effective date are at the top; important changes are announced in the game.
