Delete your account and data

App: AETHER (Google Play package com.gamegoal.aether) · Developer: Lagom Labs · Last updated: November 26, 2026

How to request deletion

  1. Open AETHER and note your in-game name. It is shown at the top of the home screen and on your Ranking row.
  2. Send an email to lagomlabs@gmail.com with the subject “AETHER account deletion”.
  3. Include your in-game name. If you linked Google, send the email from that Google address so we can match the account.
  4. We confirm by email and delete the account within 30 days.

To remove only the data stored on your phone, clear AETHER’s app data in Android settings or uninstall the app.

What we delete

  • Your Firebase account (anonymous user id and, if linked, the Google sign-in link).
  • Your cloud document: display name, email address and photo URL (if Google was linked), scores, waves, combos, kills, play time, run count, Essence balance, shop upgrades, owned and selected ships, achievements, and stored purchase tokens.
  • On request, the Firebase Analytics usage events linked to your device's app-instance id (tell us roughly when you used the app).

What is kept

  • Payment records for Essence purchases are held by Google Play under Google’s own policies; we cannot delete them.
  • The in-game leaderboard is calculated on your phone, so there is no public leaderboard row to delete; it disappears when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Advertising data collected by Google AdMob for the optional rewarded videos is handled by Google. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings.
  • We keep no backups of deleted cloud documents.
