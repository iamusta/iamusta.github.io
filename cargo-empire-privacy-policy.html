The short version: Cargo Empire has no accounts, no cloud saves and no analytics. Your progress is stored only on your phone and is never sent to us. The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob, which only appear when you choose to watch one. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play — we never see your payment details. We do not sell your data.

01

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • When you found your company you choose a company name, a founder name, an age group, a company color, an emblem and a headquarters city. These stay on your device; they are never sent to us.
  • Cargo Empire has no registration, email or password.
  • If you write to us at the contact address below, that message is handled by your email provider, not stored inside the app.

Stored locally on your device (save file user://save.json):

  • Company: the company details listed above.
  • Progress: money, vehicles and their levels, upgrades, managers, manager cards, gems, investors, regions, missions, contracts, events, the season pass and statistics.
  • Purchases: the IDs of permanent items you own so they stay unlocked.
  • Daily features: the date and streak of your daily reward and the number of ads watched today.
  • Settings: sound, music, vibration, graphics quality, daylight, season, language and notifications.

You can copy a backup code of this save file in Settings to move your progress to a new phone. The code is created on your device and only goes where you paste or send it yourself.

Collected automatically by third-party services (see sections 04–06):

  • Google AdMob: the Android Advertising ID, device and app information, IP address (and coarse location derived from it) and ad-interaction data, used to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Google Play Billing: when you buy something, Google processes the payment and returns to the app only the product ID and a purchase token.

02

How We Use Information

  • To save your company, progress, purchases and settings between sessions.
  • To show optional rewarded ads (for example free gems, double income for a while, or earnings while the game is closed) and to measure those ads.
  • To deliver the items you buy and to keep permanent purchases unlocked.
  • To turn off real-money purchases and use child-appropriate ad settings for players who select the under-13 age group.

We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not use it for anything unrelated to running Cargo Empire.

03

Device Permissions

All gameplay runs on your device. Cargo Empire does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise location.

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to load ads and talk to Google Play.
  • VIBRATE — haptic feedback (can be turned off in Settings).
  • AD_ID — added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to read the Advertising ID for ads.
  • Other technical permissions added by Google libraries (READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATE, WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE) are used only by those libraries for ad delivery.

04

Third-Party Services

Cargo Empire uses the following services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob (rewarded ads) — How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Play Billing & Google Play — Google Privacy Policy

Cargo Empire does not include analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools of its own.

05

Advertising & Your Consent

Ads appear only when you choose to watch one, for example from a bonus on the map, the x2 Turbo button or the free gems offer. Watching ads is never required to play.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the game shows Google's consent form so you can choose whether ads may be personalized. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy.
  • US states with privacy laws: Google's consent tool lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for advertising.
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

06

In-App Purchases

Gems, packs and other items bought with real money are sold through Google Play. Your payment details are processed by Google and are never shared with us or stored in the app. Real-money purchases are turned off for players who select the under-13 age group.

07

Data Storage, Retention & Security

  • Your save file stays on your device, in the app's private storage, until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.
  • We do not operate servers for Cargo Empire and do not receive or store any of your data.
  • Data collected by Google AdMob and Google Play is retained and secured according to Google's policies.
  • Notifications (daily reward, events, your fleet's shift ending) are scheduled locally on your device; you can turn them off in Settings.

08

Children's Privacy

Cargo Empire is a general-audience game. When you found your company, you choose an age group. If you select under 13:

  • Real-money purchases are turned off.
  • Ad requests are marked as child-directed and under the age of consent, so Google serves only non-personalized ads rated G (general audiences), with no interest-based targeting.

The age group is stored only on your device and used only for these purposes. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's information has been collected, contact us and we will help.

09

Your Rights

  • Access & deletion (local data): everything the app stores is visible in the game; clearing the app's data or uninstalling deletes it.
  • Advertising: change your ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy (EEA/UK/Switzerland), and reset or opt out of the Advertising ID in your device settings.
  • GDPR (EU/EEA/UK): you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
  • KVKK (Türkiye): under Law No. 6698 you may request information about, correction or deletion of your personal data by writing to us.
  • CCPA (California): Cargo Empire does not sell your personal information.
