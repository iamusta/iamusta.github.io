Lotopia: Idle Parking Tycoon — Product Delivery Agreement

Lagom Labs · version 0.1.0 (closed testing) · 18 November 2026

This document defines the scope of the Android game Lotopia: Idle Parking Tycoon (package com.lagomlabs.lotopia) and the terms under which in-app purchases are delivered. Every statement is derived from the game's source code and data files; nothing that is not in the game is listed.

1. Product

A portrait, one-handed, offline idle tycoon: the player parks cars, collects fees and grows parking lots into an empire. Platform: Android (Godot 4.7, arm64-v8a and armeabi-v7a). No accounts; progress is saved on the device. Optional rewarded ads (Google AdMob) and optional items sold through Google Play.

2. Content (verified)

  • 8 hand-made locations (Maple Street downtown, Galleria Mall, Skyport Airport, Sunset Beach, Arena Stadium, Frostpeak Resort, Dustwater Town, Neon 2099), each with 6 zones, its own vehicles, scenery, music and special event; endless seeded districts afterwards.
  • Staff (valet, cashier, cleaner, security, mechanic), entry gate and pay booth upgrades, 4 facilities per location (car wash, vending machine, EV charging, café).
  • 30 collectible managers in 4 rarities, free/ad/gem/epic chests; live events (VIP limo, breakdowns, lost wallet, rush hours and location specials, booth jams).
  • Renovate (prestige with Golden Tickets and a 7-node Ticket Tree) and Empire Rebrand (Crowns); gold-car album; daily and weekly quests, achievements, 7-day login gifts.
  • Lot Jam sliding-car puzzle: endless generated levels and a daily puzzle, undo, restart, hints.
  • Offline earnings (2 hours base, extendable by upgrades and managers up to 12 hours).
  • 78 interface languages.

4. Delivery terms

  • Consumable products are delivered immediately after Google Play confirms the purchase and its consumption.
  • Permanent products are delivered once, acknowledged with Google Play and restored automatically on reinstall or a new device signed in with the same Google account (gems are not granted again).
  • Pending payments (for example cash payments) are delivered only when Google Play reports them as completed.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional; if no ad is available nothing is lost and the button shows “not ready”.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play according to its refund policy.
