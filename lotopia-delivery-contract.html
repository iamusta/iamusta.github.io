1. Purpose

This agreement records what Lotopia v1.1.0 delivers and how purchased digital items are delivered. It is a product scope, acceptance and delivery document — not legal advice.

2. Product definition (verified)

Lotopia is a one-handed vertical idle parking tycoon: the player runs a car park, parks customers' cars, collects fees at the booth and grows from one street lot to a city-wide parking empire. All gameplay runs on the device; there are no accounts of its own. With Google Play Games the player's progress is backed up as a saved game, and leaderboards and achievements are shown in Google Play Games. Optional rewarded ads and occasional interstitial ads are served by Google AdMob, optional items are sold through Google Play, and anonymous usage statistics and crash reports go to Google Firebase.

2.2 Gameplay (verified)

  • Tap waiting cars to park them; hired valets automate parking and cashiers collect at the booth
  • 8 hand-made locations (Maple Street, Galleria Mall, Skyport Airport, Sunset Beach, Arena Stadium, Frostpeak Resort, Dustwater Town, Neon 2099), then endless generated districts
  • 5 staff roles, 4 services, 30 managers with card levels and 4 kinds of chests
  • Renovate (prestige with Golden Tickets and a Ticket Tree) and Empire Rebrand (crowns)
  • Quests, achievements, daily login rewards, a gold-car album, live events (VIP cars, rush hour, breakdowns, lost wallets)
  • Lot Jam: 300+ generated puzzle levels and a daily puzzle
  • Earnings while the game is closed: up to 2 hours, extendable to 12 hours through progression; can be doubled with a rewarded ad

  • Permissions: INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, VIBRATE, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, com.android.vending.BILLING, plus the advertising-ID and technical permissions added by Google libraries
  • Ads: Google AdMob — rewarded ads only when the player chooses to watch one; interstitial ads when switching car parks (not in the first 5 minutes, at most every 3 minutes, removed by No Ads); Google's consent form in the EEA, UK and Switzerland; content rated PG

3. Delivery of purchased items

  • Seller of record: purchases are made through Google Play; Google processes the payment under the Google Play Terms of Service.
  • Delivery: items are digital and are delivered to the player's game immediately after Google Play confirms the payment. Permanent items are acknowledged and consumable items are consumed with Google Play at delivery.
  • Permanent items (no_ads, starter_pack, income_x2) belong to the Google account used for the purchase and are restored automatically when the game is reinstalled on the same Google account; the gems, chest and ×2 time in the Starter Pack are not granted again.
  • Pending payments (for example cash payments) are delivered when Google Play confirms them, including the next time the game is opened.
  • Gems are an in-game virtual currency with no cash value. They cannot be exchanged for money, transferred or traded, and are stored on the device: clearing app data or uninstalling removes them, unless the progress is restored from the player's Google Play Games saved game.
  • Chests contain manager cards that only improve the game; their possible contents and odds are shown in the game, they hold no real-money value and cannot be cashed out.
  • Right of withdrawal: because digital content is delivered immediately at the player's request, the right of withdrawal ends once delivery begins, to the extent permitted by applicable law (including EU consumer law and the Turkish Regulation on Distance Contracts, Art. 15(ğ)).
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy. If an item was paid for but not delivered, contact us with the Google Play order number (GPA.…) and we will deliver it or help you obtain a refund.
