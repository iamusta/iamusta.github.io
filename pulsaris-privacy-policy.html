The short version: PULSARIS has no accounts, no sign-in and no cloud save. Your progress is stored only on your phone. The game shows optional rewarded ads (Google AdMob) and uses Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play — we never see your payment details. We do not sell your data.

01

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • None. PULSARIS has no registration, account, email, password or sign-in.
  • If you write to us at the contact address below, that message is handled by your email provider, not stored inside the app.

Stored locally on your device (save file user://save.json):

  • Progress: credit balance, best score, farthest sector, total runs and kills, owned and selected ships, paints, permanent upgrades, booster stock and on/off state.
  • Purchases: the IDs of permanent store items you own (e.g. no_ads, starter_pack) so they stay unlocked.
  • Daily features: the date and streak of your daily reward and today's daily missions with their progress.
  • Settings: music, sound effects, vibration, language and control sensitivity.

Collected automatically by third-party services (see sections 04–06):

  • Google AdMob: the Android Advertising ID, device and app information, IP address and ad-interaction data, used to deliver and measure ads.
  • Firebase Analytics: an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system, app version, coarse (country-level) location derived from the IP address, and the in-game events listed in section 05.
  • Google Play Billing: when you buy something, Google processes the payment and returns to the app only the product ID and a purchase token.

02

How We Use Information

  • To save your progress, unlocks, purchases and settings between sessions.
  • To show optional rewarded ads that let you continue a run, and to measure those ads.
  • To deliver the items you buy and to restore permanent purchases.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the game is played (e.g. where players stop, which features are used) and to balance and improve it.

We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not use it for anything unrelated to running and improving PULSARIS.

03

Gameplay & Device Permissions

All gameplay runs on your device. PULSARIS does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise location.

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to load ads, send analytics and talk to Google Play.
  • VIBRATE — haptic feedback (can be turned off in Settings).
  • AD_ID and related advertising permissions added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK — to read the Advertising ID for ads.
  • Other technical permissions added by Google libraries (e.g. Wi-Fi state, wake lock, install referrer) are used only by those libraries for ad delivery and analytics.

04

Third-Party Services

PULSARIS uses the following services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob (rewarded ads) — How Google uses information in ads
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — Firebase privacy
  • Google Play Billing & Google Play — Google Privacy Policy

05

Analytics

We send the following gameplay events to Firebase Analytics. They contain game data only (no names, emails or free text):

  • Run start and end (ship, score, sector reached, kills, credits earned, run time).
  • Sector cleared; daily reward and mission claimed; language changed.
  • Ship, paint, upgrade and booster bought with in-game credits.
  • Rewarded ad requested and completed; store purchase started, completed or failed; purchases restored.

PULSARIS does not include crash-reporting tools.

06

Advertising & In-App Purchases

Advertising. Ads appear only when you choose to watch one (for example, "Continue · Watch Ad" on the game-over screen). Google AdMob may use your Advertising ID to show personalised ads. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

In-app purchases. PULSARIS offers optional items through Google Play:

  • No Ads (permanent) — continue for free without watching ads, plus 5,000 credits.
  • Starter Pack (permanent, one time) — VIPER ship, 3,000 credits and 3 boosters.
  • Credit packs (consumable) — 2,500, 8,000 or 25,000 credits.

Payments are processed by Google Play. We never receive your card or bank details. There are no subscriptions.

07

Data Storage, Retention & Security

  • Your save file stays in the app's private storage on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Android's backup feature may include it in your device backup.
  • Analytics data is kept by Firebase according to our project's retention setting (no longer than 14 months) and is then deleted or aggregated.
  • Ad data is retained by Google under its own policies.
  • Data sent to Google services is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

08

Children's Privacy

PULSARIS is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and want data removed, contact us and we will help.

09

Your Rights

  • Access & deletion (local data): everything the app stores is visible in the game; clearing the app's data or uninstalling deletes it.
  • Analytics data: email us to request access to or deletion of analytics data linked to your app-instance; you can also stop collection by uninstalling the app.
  • Advertising: reset or opt out of the Advertising ID in your device settings.
  • GDPR (EU/EEA/UK): you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
  • KVKK (Türkiye): under Law No. 6698 you may request information about, correction or deletion of your personal data by writing to us.
  • CCPA (California): PULSARIS does not sell your personal information.
