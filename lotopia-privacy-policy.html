Lotopia: Idle Parking Tycoon

Privacy Policy · Lagom Labs · Effective 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Lotopia: Idle Parking Tycoon (package com.lagomlabs.lotopia, “the Game”) published by Lagom Labs (“we”) processes, why, and your choices. The Game has no accounts, no chat and no user-generated content. It is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other directly identifying information. Your game progress is stored only on your device.

2. Information processed by services in the Game

3. Your choices

  • Ads are optional rewards. You can play the whole Game without watching rewarded videos. The “No Ads” purchase removes interstitial ads.
  • Consent. Where required, a consent form appears on first launch; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Notifications. Local reminders (offline earnings full, free chest, daily puzzle) can be turned off in Settings or in Android settings.

4. Retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Analytics data is kept by Firebase for up to 14 months; crash reports for up to 90 days. To request deletion of analytics or crash data associated with your installation, e-mail us with the approximate install date; we will forward the request to Google.

5. Security

All data sent by the services above is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes

We will update this page if the Game’s data practices change and show the new effective date above.
