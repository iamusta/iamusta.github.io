The short version: Lotopia has no accounts. Your progress is stored only on your phone and is never sent to us. The game collects anonymous usage statistics and crash reports through Google Firebase (you can turn usage statistics off in Settings). It shows ads through Google AdMob: rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one, and occasional full-screen ads when you switch car parks. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play — we never see your payment details. We do not sell your data.

01

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly: none. Lotopia has no registration, name, email or password. If you write to us at the contact address below, that message is handled by your email provider, not stored inside the app.

Stored locally on your device (save file in the app's private storage, with an automatic backup copy):

  • Progress: money, gems, car parks and their spots, zones, gates, booths, staff, services, managers and manager cards, Golden Tickets and the Ticket Tree, Empire Rebrand crowns, quests, achievements, the gold-car album, Lot Jam puzzle progress and statistics.
  • Purchases: the IDs of permanent items you own so they stay unlocked.
  • Daily features: login reward streak, free chest timer and the number of ads watched today.
  • Settings: music, sound effects, vibration, notifications, language and usage statistics.

Collected automatically by third-party services (see sections 04–06):

  • Google AdMob: the Android Advertising ID, device and app information, IP address (and coarse location derived from it) and ad-interaction data, used to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Google Play Billing: when you buy something, Google processes the payment and returns to the app only the product ID and a purchase token.
  • Google Firebase Analytics (usage statistics): a random app-instance ID created by Firebase, device model, OS version, language, country (from the IP address, which is not stored) and in-game events such as tutorial steps, unlocking a zone or car park, hiring staff, renovating a car park, opening the welcome-back screen, tapping a bonus bubble, watching an ad and completing a purchase (product ID only). Firebase is configured not to collect the Android ID (SSAID) and not to send ad-personalization signals; these statistics are never used for advertising. Turn them off any time in Settings → Usage statistics.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports): if the game crashes, a report with the stack trace, device model, OS version, app version and the app-instance ID is sent so we can fix the problem.
  • Google Firebase Remote Config: the game downloads game-balance values (for example how often ads may appear); this request contains the app-instance ID and app version.
  • Google Play In-App Review: the game may ask Google Play to show its rating dialog; Google handles the dialog and any review you write.

02

How We Use Information

  • To save your progress, purchases and settings between sessions.
  • To show ads: optional rewarded ads (for example free gems, ×2 income for a while, a bonus bubble or doubling your welcome-back earnings) and occasional full-screen ads between car parks, and to measure those ads.
  • To deliver the items you buy and to keep permanent purchases unlocked.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the game is played and where it crashes, so we can improve balance and fix problems.
  • To send local reminders on your device (see section 03); these are created by the app on your phone, not sent from a server.

We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not use it for anything unrelated to running Lotopia.

03

Device Permissions

All gameplay runs on your device. Lotopia does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise location.

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to load ads, talk to Google Play and Firebase.
  • VIBRATE — haptic feedback (can be turned off in Settings).
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — local reminders (your car park is full of cash, the free chest is ready, a daily reminder); Android asks before showing any, and you can turn them off in Settings.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — to keep those reminders scheduled after the phone restarts.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING — in-app purchases through Google Play.
  • AD_ID — used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to read the Advertising ID for ads.
  • Other technical permissions added by Google libraries (for example WAKE_LOCK) are used only by those libraries.

04

Third-Party Services

Lotopia uses the following services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob (ads) — How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config — Privacy and Security in Firebase
  • Google Play Billing, Google Play In-App Review & Google Play — Google Privacy Policy

Lotopia does not include analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools of its own.

05

Advertising & Your Consent

Rewarded ads appear only when you choose to watch one (free gems, ×2 income, bonus bubbles, doubling welcome-back earnings, an ad chest). Watching ads is never required to play. Full-screen (interstitial) ads may appear when you switch between car parks — never in the first 5 minutes of a session and at most once every 3 minutes. The No Ads purchase removes them.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the game shows Google's consent form so you can choose whether ads may be personalized. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • US states with privacy laws: Google's consent tool lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for advertising.
  • Ad requests are limited to content rated PG and are not marked child-directed (the game is for ages 13+).
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

06

In-App Purchases

Gems, packs and permanent upgrades are sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own terms; we receive only the product ID and a purchase token, never your name, card or billing details. Delivery of purchased items is described in our Delivery Agreement.

07

Data Storage, Retention & Security

  • Your save stays in the app's private storage on your phone until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. It is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Firebase Analytics data is kept by Google for up to 14 months; Crashlytics crash reports for up to 90 days.
  • All network traffic from the game (ads, Google Play, Firebase) is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

08

Children's Privacy

Lotopia is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's information has been collected, contact us and we will help.

09

Your Rights

  • Access & deletion (local data): everything the app stores is visible in the game; clearing the app's data or uninstalling deletes it.
  • Usage statistics: turn off Firebase Analytics in Settings → Usage statistics at any time.
  • Advertising: change your ad consent in Settings → Privacy options (EEA/UK/Switzerland), and reset or opt out of the Advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Deletion requests: write to us and we will ask Google to delete the analytics and crash data linked to your app-instance ID (you can find it by contacting us from the device).
  • GDPR (EU/EEA/UK): you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
  • KVKK (Türkiye): under Law No. 6698 you may request information about, correction or deletion of your personal data by writing to us.
  • CCPA (California): Lotopia does not sell your personal information.
