1. Purpose

This agreement records what PULSARIS v3.0 delivers at release and how purchased digital items are delivered. It is a product scope, acceptance and delivery document — not legal advice.

2. Product definition (verified)

PULSARIS is a one-finger endless vertical space shooter. All gameplay runs on the device; there are no accounts and no cloud save. Optional rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob, gameplay events are sent to Firebase Analytics, and optional items are sold through Google Play Billing.

2.2 Gameplay (verified)

  • Drag to steer; firing is automatic
  • 6 themed sectors repeat endlessly with rising difficulty; each ends with a three-phase boss
  • 6 enemy types; power-ups: weapon level, shield, rapid fire, magnet, nova bomb, repair
  • 9 ships in 4 rarity tiers, 7 paints, 10 permanent upgrades, 4 single-use boosters — all bought with in-game credits

2.5 Permissions, ads & analytics

  • Permissions: INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, VIBRATE, plus advertising-ID and technical permissions added by Google libraries
  • Ads: rewarded only, shown only when the player chooses to watch one
  • Analytics: Firebase Analytics gameplay events (see the Privacy Policy)
  • Account & cloud: none — progress is saved locally on the device

3. Delivery of purchased items

  • Seller of record: purchases are made through Google Play; Google processes the payment under the Google Play Terms of Service.
  • Delivery: items are digital and are delivered to the player's game immediately after Google Play confirms the payment. Permanent items are acknowledged and consumable credit packs are consumed with Google Play at delivery.
  • Permanent items (no_ads, starter_pack) can be restored on the same Google account with Store → Restore purchases.
  • Credits are an in-game virtual currency with no cash value. They cannot be exchanged for money, transferred or traded, and are stored on the device: clearing app data or uninstalling removes them.
  • Right of withdrawal: because digital content is delivered immediately at the player's request, the right of withdrawal ends once delivery begins, to the extent permitted by applicable law (including EU consumer law and Turkish Regulation on Distance Contracts, Art. 15(ğ)).
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy. If an item was paid for but not delivered, contact us with the Google Play order number (GPA.…) and we will deliver it or help you obtain a refund.
