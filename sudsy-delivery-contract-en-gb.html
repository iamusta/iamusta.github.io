Products are digital. A product is added to your game as soon as Google Play confirms the payment. There is no physical delivery.

If the connection breaks during a purchase, the product is delivered the next time you open the game, exactly once. Permanent products can also be restored with Settings → Restore purchases. If something is still missing, write to lagomlabs@gmail.com with your Google Play order number.

All payments are taken by Google Play under Google Play's terms. The game never sees or stores your card or bank details.
