1. Purpose

This agreement records what Stage Up v0.9.2 delivers and how purchased digital items are delivered. It is a product scope, acceptance and delivery document — not legal advice.

2. Product definition (verified)

Stage Up is a casual rocket-launch game: the player upgrades a rocket in the workshop, launches it with three timing moments (launch gauge, stage separation, parachute), earns gold by the altitude reached and climbs from the first kilometre to Jupiter. All gameplay runs on the device; there are no accounts of its own. With Google Play Games the player's progress can be backed up as a saved game and achievements are shown in Google Play Games. Optional rewarded ads and occasional interstitial ads are served by Google AdMob, optional items are sold through Google Play, and usage statistics and crash reports go to Google Firebase.

2.2 Gameplay (verified)

  • Four upgradeable parts (engine, fuel tank, body, stages up to 4) and 15 rocket models from the free Classic to the Titan
  • Eight targets from 1 km to Jupiter (588 million km), each with a gold reward
  • Daily missions with a bonus chest, a 7-day daily reward, 38 achievements with gold rewards
  • Sponsors: invented brands offer a fee per flight; the player can sign, ask for more or decline, and a sponsor may walk away
  • A crash keeps part of the flight's gold as a repair cost (25% by default); a safe parachute landing adds a recovery bonus
  • Earnings while the game is closed, up to 8 hours, which can be doubled with a rewarded ad
  • Local reminders at most once or twice a day, which the player can turn off

2.5 Permissions, ads & analytics

  • Permissions: INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, VIBRATE, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, com.android.vending.BILLING, plus the advertising-ID and technical permissions added by Google libraries
  • Ads: Google AdMob — rewarded ads only when the player chooses to watch one; interstitial ads after some flights (not in the first flights, removed by Remove Ads); Google's consent form in the EEA, UK and Switzerland; content rated PG
  • Analytics: Google Firebase Analytics (can be turned off in Settings), Crashlytics crash reports, Remote Config
  • Account & cloud: no own accounts; optional Google Play Games sign-in for the cloud saved game and achievements

3. Delivery of purchased items

  • Seller of record: purchases are made through Google Play; Google processes the payment under the Google Play Terms of Service.
  • Delivery: items are digital and are delivered to the player's game immediately after Google Play confirms the payment. Permanent items are acknowledged and consumable items are consumed with Google Play at delivery.
  • Permanent items (starter_pack, no_ads, vip_engineer, double_gold) belong to the Google account used for the purchase and are restored when the game is reinstalled on the same Google account (Shop → Restore purchases); the gold, rocket and ×2 flights in the Starter Pack and the VIP gold gift are not granted again.
  • Pending payments (for example cash payments) are delivered when Google Play confirms them, including the next time the game is opened.
  • Gold is an in-game virtual currency with no cash value. It cannot be exchanged for money, transferred or traded, and is stored on the device: clearing app data or uninstalling removes it, unless the progress is restored from the player's Google Play Games saved game.
  • Right of withdrawal: because digital content is delivered immediately at the player's request, the right of withdrawal ends once delivery begins, to the extent permitted by applicable law (including EU consumer law and the Turkish Regulation on Distance Contracts, Art. 15(ğ)).
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy. If an item was paid for but not delivered, contact us with the Google Play order number (GPA.…) and we will deliver it or help you obtain a refund.
