01Parties & Scope

This agreement is between LagomLabs (the developer, contact lagomlabs@gmail.com) and the player who installs Saha Kralı: Halı Saha Tycoon (package com.lagomlabs.football.pitch.free.games.tycoon) from Google Play. It describes the product and the terms under which in-app purchases are delivered. Statements are derived from the app's source code.

02Product

Saha Kralı: Halı Saha Tycoon is a one-handed, vertical idle football-pitch business game. The player runs five-a-side pitches, sets hourly prices, buys upgrades, hires staff and opens branches. All gameplay runs on the device.

  • Branches (7): Eski Mahalle, Organize Sanayi, Kampüs, Sahil Bandı, Site İçi, Plaza Bölgesi, Olimpik Köy
  • Pitch upgrades: Suni Çim, Kale & File, Projektör, Çevre Filesi, Elektronik Skorbord, Ses Sistemi, Maç Kaydı Kamerası
  • Facility upgrades: Kantin & Çay Ocağı, Soyunma Odası, Duş & Sıcak Su, Otopark, Tribün, Kapalı Çatı & Isıtma
  • Staff: Saha Görevlisi, Temizlik Görevlisi, Kantinci, Sosyal Medya Sorumlusu, Hakem, Güvenlik — wages are paid every second; if the till is empty, staff go on strike until it recovers
  • A fast in-game clock (1 game hour = 1 minute): demand follows the time of day, weekends and the weather; a covered roof protects against rain and snow
  • Build a chain (prestige) for fans that permanently raise income; daily rewards, missions, a live pitch view
  • Earnings while the game is closed: the first hour is free, up to 8 more hours with a rewarded ad, or always with Night Watchman

04Gem Items (no real money)

Gems are an in-game virtual currency with no cash value. They are earned in the game or bought, and spent on items such as Turbo (2x income for a while), wage exemption, time warps and permanent perks. They cannot be exchanged for money, transferred or traded.

05Ordering, Payment & Seller

  • Purchases are made through Google Play, which processes payment under the Google Play Terms of Service.
  • The price including taxes is shown by Google Play before you confirm.

06Delivery

  • Items are digital and are delivered to the game immediately after Google Play confirms the payment.
  • Permanent items belong to the Google account used to buy them and are restored when the game is reinstalled on the same account; gems and Turbo time in the Starter Pack are not granted again.
  • Pending payments are delivered when Google Play confirms them, including the next time the game is opened.
  • Gems are stored on the device: clearing app data or uninstalling removes them unless a backup or cloud save is restored.

07Right of Withdrawal & Refunds

  • Because digital content is delivered immediately at your request, the right of withdrawal ends once delivery begins, to the extent permitted by law (EU consumer law; Turkish Regulation on Distance Contracts, Art. 15(1)(ğ)).
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy.
  • If an item was paid for but not delivered, write to lagomlabs@gmail.com with your Google Play order number (GPA.…); we will deliver it or help you obtain a refund.

08Advertising

Ads are optional rewarded ads only (Google AdMob), shown when you choose to watch one; the reward is granted after the ad is watched. See the Privacy Policy for consent and children's settings.

09Availability & Changes

We may update the game to fix problems, rebalance or add content. Permanent items you bought keep their effect or, if an item is removed, an equivalent replacement is provided. Your statutory consumer rights are not limited by this agreement.

10Governing Law & Contact

Mandatory consumer law of your country of residence applies. For Turkish consumers, consumer arbitration committees and consumer courts are competent. Contact: lagomlabs@gmail.com. Privacy Policy: https://iamusta.github.io/saha-krali-privacy-policy.html.

02Ürün

Saha Kralı: Halı Saha Tycoon tek elle, dikey oynanan bir idle halı saha işletme oyunudur. Oyuncu sahaları işletir, saatlik fiyatı belirler, yükseltme alır, personel tutar ve şube açar. Oyun cihazda çalışır.

  • Şubeler (7): Eski Mahalle, Organize Sanayi, Kampüs, Sahil Bandı, Site İçi, Plaza Bölgesi, Olimpik Köy
  • Saha yükseltmeleri: Suni Çim, Kale & File, Projektör, Çevre Filesi, Elektronik Skorbord, Ses Sistemi, Maç Kaydı Kamerası
  • Tesis yükseltmeleri: Kantin & Çay Ocağı, Soyunma Odası, Duş & Sıcak Su, Otopark, Tribün, Kapalı Çatı & Isıtma
  • Personel: Saha Görevlisi, Temizlik Görevlisi, Kantinci, Sosyal Medya Sorumlusu, Hakem, Güvenlik — maaşlar her saniye ödenir; kasa boşalırsa personel kasa toparlanana kadar greve çıkar
  • Hızlı oyun saati (1 oyun saati = 1 dakika): talep günün saatine, hafta sonuna ve havaya göre değişir; Kapalı Çatı yağmur ve kara karşı korur
  • Zincir Kur (prestij) ile gelirini kalıcı artıran taraftarlar; günlük ödül, görevler, canlı saha görünümü
  • Oyun kapalıyken kazanç: ilk saat bedava, ödüllü reklamla 8 saat daha ya da Gece Bekçisi ile her zaman
